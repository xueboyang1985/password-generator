What Makes a Password Strong?

A strong password has three key characteristics:

  • Length: At least 12 characters, ideally 16 or more. Every additional character exponentially increases cracking time.
  • Complexity: Mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Unpredictability: No dictionary words, personal information, or recognizable patterns.

Common Password Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using personal information: Birthdays, names, pet names, or phone numbers are easily guessed or found on social media.
  • Reusing passwords: If one site gets breached, all your accounts are compromised. Use a unique password everywhere.
  • Dictionary words: Even with substitutions like "p@ssw0rd", modern cracking tools check these variations.
  • Keyboard patterns: "qwerty123" or "asdfgh" are among the first patterns checked.
  • Short passwords: Anything under 8 characters can be cracked in minutes regardless of complexity.

How Password Cracking Works

Understanding how attackers crack passwords helps you defend against them:

  • Brute force: Trying every possible combination. An 8-character password has 208 billion possibilities — but a modern GPU can check billions per second.
  • Dictionary attacks: Trying common words, phrases, and known password patterns.
  • Rainbow tables: Pre-computed hash tables for quick reverse-lookup of common passwords.
  • Social engineering: Using personal information gathered from social media to guess passwords.

Password Strength Comparison

  • 6 characters (letters only): Cracked instantly
  • 8 characters (mixed): Cracked in ~8 hours
  • 12 characters (mixed): Cracked in ~34 years
  • 16 characters (mixed + symbols): Cracked in ~44,000 years

Tips for Managing Strong Passwords

What is the minimum password length for security?

Security experts recommend at least 12 characters. Shorter passwords, even with complex characters, can be cracked relatively quickly with modern hardware.

Should I use special characters in my password?

Yes. Adding symbols (!@#$%^&*) significantly increases the character set size, making brute-force attacks exponentially more difficult.

How often should I change my password?

Unless you suspect your password has been compromised, changing it every 6-12 months is sufficient. Frequent changes often lead to weaker passwords.
